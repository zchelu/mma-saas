Privacy Policy

KombatDesk LLC · Last updated: July 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy for KombatDesk LLC ("KombatDesk," "we," "us," or "our"), a Colorado limited liability company doing business as KombatDesk, describes how and why we collect, use, store, and share ("process") personal information when you use our services (the "Services"), including when you:

  • Visit our website at www.kombatdesk.com or any of our pages that link to this Privacy Policy;
  • Create an account for, subscribe to, or use the KombatDesk platform — a gym management platform for independent MMA and BJJ gym owners that provides member management, class scheduling and attendance tracking, a check-in kiosk, SMS messaging related to membership and attendance, and subscription billing; or
  • Engage with us in other related ways, including sales calls, demos, and customer support.

This Privacy Policy applies to two groups of people: gym owners (our customers, who create accounts and subscribe to the Services) and gym members (individuals whose information is entered into the Services by their gym). If you are a gym member, please also read Section 4, "Member Information and the Member Opt-In Page," which explains how your information reaches us and who is responsible for it.

Reading this Privacy Policy will help you understand your privacy rights and choices. If you do not agree with our policies and practices, please do not use our Services. If you still have questions or concerns, contact us at kombatdesk@outlook.com.

  • We do not collect sensitive personal information such as health data, biometric identifiers, government ID numbers, or precise geolocation.
  • Service providers only: We disclose personal information only to service providers that process it on our behalf to run the Services (listed by name in Section 6), and as required by law.
  • SMS messages: The Services send text messages related to membership and attendance. Automatic messages are limited to one per member per 7-day period. Reply STOP at any time to opt out. See Section 9.
  • Your rights: Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or obtain a copy of your personal information. See Sections 13 and 14.

1. What Information Do We Collect?

Personal information you provide to us

We collect personal information that you voluntarily provide when you register for the Services, subscribe to a plan, or contact us. This includes:

  • Contact and account information: your name, email address, and account credentials managed through our authentication provider (we do not store raw passwords ourselves — see Section 6);
  • Gym/business information: your gym's name and related account details;
  • Billing information: your billing address and payment card details, which are collected and processed directly by our payment processor, Stripe. KombatDesk does not receive or store full payment card numbers;
  • Communications: information you provide when you contact support, participate in a demo, or otherwise correspond with us.

All personal information that you provide to us must be true, complete, and accurate, and you must notify us of any changes to it.

2. Information Collected Automatically

We do not use a product analytics service, an advertising network, or any cross-site tracking technology. The information collected automatically when you use the Services is limited to what is needed to deliver and protect them:

  • IP address: the network address a request comes from. We use it in two ways. First, to apply rate limits that protect our checkout, account-recovery, check-in, and member opt-in pages from automated abuse; these rate-limit counters are stored keyed to the IP address and are not linked to an account. Second, where a gym member opts in to text messages through our member opt-in page, we record the IP address the opt-in was submitted from as part of that member's consent record (see Section 4).
  • Browser user agent: the identifying string your browser sends with a request. Where a gym member opts in to text messages through our member opt-in page, we record it as part of that member's consent record (see Section 4).
  • Request and server logs: our hosting and backend providers (Vercel and Convex — see Section 6) process standard request metadata, including IP address, timestamp, and the page or endpoint requested, in order to route traffic, keep the Services available, and investigate errors and abuse.

We do not infer your location from your IP address, we do not collect precise geolocation (such as GPS coordinates), and we never request access to your device's location services. See Section 7 for information about cookies and similar technologies.

3. Information from Google Sign-In

Our Services allow you to register and sign in using your Google account. When you choose to sign in with Google, we receive basic profile information from Google, such as your name and email address, which we use solely to create and authenticate your KombatDesk account.

KombatDesk's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We do not use information received from Google APIs for advertising, and we do not sell it or transfer it to third parties except as necessary to provide and improve the sign-in functionality described here.

4. Member Information and the Member Opt-In Page

If you are a gym member: most of your information reaches KombatDesk through your gym rather than from you directly — but there is one page where you may give information to us yourself. This section explains both.

Information your gym gives us. Most personal information we hold about gym members is entered by the gym or academy account holder ("Customer"). It may include names, phone numbers, email addresses, membership plan and status, belt rank and promotion history, class enrollments, attendance and check-in records, and records of SMS consent and opt-out status. We also derive, from a member's attendance record, whether that member appears to have stopped attending, so that their gym can follow up with them; where a member returns after receiving a message, we record that return.

Information you give us directly through the member opt-in page. KombatDesk hosts an opt-in page for each gym, at a web address of the form kombatdesk.com/consent/<your-gym>, which gyms share with their members. If you use that page, you submit your name and phone number to KombatDesk directly. When you do, we also record the date and time of your submission, the exact consent wording that was displayed to you and the version of that wording, the IP address the submission came from, and your browser's user agent string. We keep that record as evidence that an opt-in took place. It is stored by KombatDesk on our own systems, and we will provide a copy of it to your gym, or to you, on request.

The Customer is responsible for providing members with any privacy notices required under applicable law and for obtaining any consents necessary before submitting member information to KombatDesk — including express consent to receive text messages, as described in Section 9 and in our Terms of Service. KombatDesk processes member information solely on the Customer's behalf and at the Customer's direction, consistent with the Terms of Service. Members with questions about how their information is used should contact their gym directly; KombatDesk will assist Customers in responding to member requests where required by law.

9. SMS Text Messaging

The Services send SMS text messages to gym members on behalf of their gym. These messages are membership and attendance-related notices and replies to messages a member sends us — for example, a check-in message when a member has not attended class recently. We do not send marketing or promotional text messages.

  • Consent: A member's number is only messaged once consent has been recorded, by one of two routes: the member opts in themselves through the member opt-in page described in Section 4, or the gym confirms to us that it has already obtained that member's consent directly. Where the gym confirms it, the gym is responsible for having obtained that consent, as described in Section 4 and in our Terms of Service.
  • Frequency: Automatic messages are limited to at most one message per member per 7-day period (approximately 4 per month). Gyms can also send messages to opted-in members manually; the frequency of those messages is set by the gym and is not capped by KombatDesk.
  • The number messages are sent from: KombatDesk currently sends messages for every gym on its platform from a single shared phone number. There is a practical consequence you should know about. Because opting out is tied to that phone number, replying STOP stops messages from every gym that uses KombatDesk, not only the gym whose message you received — and replying START resumes them for every such gym. If you train at more than one gym that uses KombatDesk and want to stop messages from only one of them, contact that gym directly.
  • Opt-out: Members may opt out at any time by replying STOP, STOPALL, UNSUBSCRIBE, CANCEL, END, or QUIT to any message, and may opt back in by replying START, YES, or UNSTOP. Opt-out status is recorded and honored across the Services. Members can reply HELP for assistance, or contact us using the details in Section 18.
  • Rates: Message and data rates may apply, depending on the member's mobile carrier and plan.

Phone numbers and SMS consent status are shared with Twilio solely to deliver messages, and are not used for any other purpose or shared with any other party for their own use.

10. How Long Do We Keep Your Information?

We keep personal information for as long as the associated account remains open with us, and thereafter only as long as necessary to comply with our legal obligations (for example, tax and accounting requirements and records of SMS consent and opt-outs), resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements.

SMS consent and opt-out records. Where a member opts in through the member opt-in page, the consent record described in Section 4 — including the wording shown, the date and time, the IP address, and the browser user agent — is kept for as long as we may need it to show that the opt-in took place. Opt-out records are kept indefinitely, so that an opt-out continues to be honored even if the underlying member record is deleted.

Rate-limiting records. The abuse-prevention counters described in Section 2 are keyed to an IP address and are not linked to an account. They are kept on our backend for security purposes only.

You may request deletion of your personal information at any time as described in Section 19. When a gym's account is closed, the gym may request deletion of its data, including its members' records; we will honor such requests except where retention is required by law (for example, we retain SMS opt-out records so that opt-outs continue to be honored).

11. How Do We Keep Your Information Safe?

We have implemented appropriate and reasonable technical and organizational security measures designed to protect the personal information we process, proportionate to our size and the nature of the data. These measures include:

  • Access controls: every gym's data is isolated to that gym's account, and access rules are enforced on our servers — not just in the user interface. There is one deliberate exception, described in Section 9: an SMS opt-out is applied to every record holding that phone number, across gyms, so that an opt-out is never missed;
  • Authentication: account credentials are managed by our dedicated authentication provider (Clerk); KombatDesk does not store raw passwords;
  • Verified integrations: inbound requests from our payment and messaging providers are cryptographically verified before being processed;
  • Abuse prevention: rate limiting and input validation on our application endpoints;
  • Infrastructure: the Services run on established managed platforms that encrypt data in transit and at rest;
  • Payment isolation: full payment card numbers are handled exclusively by Stripe and never touch our systems.

However, no electronic transmission over the internet or information storage technology can be guaranteed to be 100% secure, so we cannot promise that unauthorized third parties will never be able to defeat our security. Transmission of personal information to and from our Services is at your own risk; you should access the Services only within a secure environment.

12. Do We Collect Information from Minors?

The Services are a business tool intended for use by gym owners who are at least 18 years old. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from, and the Services are not directed to, children.

Gyms may enter roster information about members who are minors (for example, youth program participants). In that case, the gym — as the party with the direct relationship with the member and their family — is responsible for obtaining any parental or guardian consent required under applicable law, including laws that protect minors under 18 such as Colorado law, before submitting a minor's information or enrolling a minor's phone number for text messages.

If we learn that we have collected personal information directly from a child in violation of this policy, we will delete it promptly. If you believe this has occurred, contact us at kombatdesk@outlook.com.

13. What Are Your Privacy Rights?

Depending on where you live, you may have some or all of the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • The right to correct inaccuracies in your personal information;
  • The right to delete your personal information;

  • The right to opt out of the sale of personal information, targeted advertising, and certain profiling — noting that we do not engage in any of these practices;

Exercising your rights

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details in Section 18 — email is the fastest route. We will need to verify your identity before acting on a request, which we typically do by matching the request to the email address or phone number associated with the account or member record. An authorized agent may submit a request on your behalf with proof of authorization; we may still need to verify your identity directly.

We will respond to verifiable requests within the timeframe required by applicable law (generally within 45 days, with a permitted extension where reasonably necessary, in which case we will tell you).

Members

If you are a gym member, we may refer your request to your gym or work with your gym to fulfill it, since your gym controls the roster in which your information appears. You may always opt out of text messages directly by replying STOP, without going through your gym.

Appeals

If we decline to take action on your request, you may appeal our decision by replying to our response or emailing kombatdesk@outlook.com with the subject line "Privacy Appeal." We will respond to appeals within the timeframe required by applicable law. If your appeal is denied and you are a Colorado resident, you may contact the Colorado Attorney General at coag.gov; residents of other states may contact their state Attorney General.

15. Do-Not-Track and Opt-Out Preference Signals

Most web browsers include a Do-Not-Track ("DNT") feature. No uniform technology standard for recognizing and implementing DNT signals has been finalized, and we do not currently respond to DNT browser signals.

Some browsers also transmit opt-out preference signals such as the Global Privacy Control ("GPC"). Because we do not sell personal information or process it for targeted advertising, there is no sale or targeted-advertising activity for such signals to opt you out of. If our practices ever change, we will update this Privacy Policy and honor applicable opt-out preference signals as required by law.
