This Cookie Policy explains how KombatDesk LLC ("KombatDesk," "we," "us," or "our") uses cookies and similar technologies when you visit our website at www.kombatdesk.com and use our services (together, the "Services"). It explains what these technologies are, why we use them, and your choices for controlling them. This Cookie Policy is part of, and should be read together with, our Privacy Policy.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small data files placed on your computer or mobile device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work, keep users signed in, protect against fraud and abuse, and provide usage reporting. Related technologies — such as local storage — serve similar purposes, and we use the word "cookies" in this policy to cover them as well.

Cookies set by the website owner (here, KombatDesk) are called "first-party cookies." Cookies set by our service providers in the course of delivering the Services (for example, our authentication and payment providers) are called "third-party cookies."

What we use cookies for — and what we don't

We use cookies for three limited purposes:

  • Authentication and session management — keeping you securely signed in to your account (set by our authentication provider, Clerk);
  • Security, fraud prevention, and bot protection — protecting sign-in and checkout against automated abuse (set by Clerk's and Stripe's security infrastructure, including Cloudflare and hCaptcha);
  • Analytics — understanding how the Services are used so we can improve them (first-party analytics via PostHog).

What we don't use: We do not use advertising, targeting, or retargeting cookies. We do not use social media cookies, pixels, or plugins. We do not sell personal information collected through cookies, and we do not use cookies to track you across other websites for advertising. We do not collect sensitive personal information or knowingly collect children's data through cookies.

Do-Not-Track and opt-out preference signals

Because we do not use advertising or targeting cookies, do not sell personal information, and do not process personal data for targeted advertising, opt-out preference signals such as the Global Privacy Control ("GPC") and browser Do-Not-Track settings do not change how we process your data — there is no advertising or sale activity to opt out of. If our practices ever change, we will update this policy and our Privacy Policy and honor applicable opt-out signals as required by law, including Colorado's universal opt-out mechanism requirements.
